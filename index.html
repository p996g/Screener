This page publishes the latest run of the screener automatically (see .github/workflows/screener.yml). It updates the first time the scheduled workflow actually fires during market hours (weekdays, ~30 min before the open and ~30 min after the close, America/New_York) — check back after that, or trigger a run manually from the repo's Actions tab.

This screener finds candidates by fixed rules. It does not know news, context, or whether a rule stopped making sense. Candidates feed a research process; they are not signals.
